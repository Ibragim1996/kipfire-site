Questions and answers

Straight answers about getting content ideas, filming yourself, and using KipFire.

Content ideas

How do I get content ideas every day?

Stop waiting for inspiration and use prompts. KipFire gives you one short card to film whenever you ask: a question, a challenge, a hot take, a would-you-rather, a funny line, or an honest line about yourself. You get a new card, react on camera, and post. It works for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

What should I post on Reels or TikTok when I have no ideas?

Post a reaction to a prompt. Simple formats work well for beginners: answer a question on camera, pick a side in a would-you-rather, share a hot take, or try a small challenge. These videos are short, easy to film, and give people something to comment on. KipFire hands you a prompt in each of these formats.

I'm scared to film myself. What do I do?

Don't try to perform. Pick one question and answer it out loud like you're talking to a friend. Keep it under 30 seconds. With KipFire the card text sits on the video, so viewers understand the video even if you just react. Most people find the second video much easier than the first.

How do I start posting as a complete beginner?

Start small and lower the bar. Film one short video with one clear point, post it, and do it again. You don't need a niche, a script or editing skills to begin. A prompt app like KipFire removes the hardest part, deciding what to say.

What do I post if I don't have a talent or skill to show?

Your opinions and reactions are enough. Questions, hot takes and would-you-rather videos are built on what you think, not on what you can do. They also get comments, because people want to answer too.

What is KipFire?

KipFire is an iPhone and iPad app that gives you short prompts to film for social media. There are six categories: Funny, Challenges, Questions, Would You Rather, Hot Take and Inner Me, plus For You, which mixes them around a niche you pick. Cards come in English and Spanish.

Does KipFire use AI to make my content?

KipFire uses AI to write the prompt, not the video. You film yourself, so the content is really you. The goal is more real human content, not more AI content.

Is KipFire free?

You can start for free. Guests get 5 tasks a day in Funny and Challenges. A free account gets 10 tasks a day and all six categories. Premium removes the daily limit.

Plans and billing

What's included in the free tier?

  • Free account: 10 tasks a day, shared across all six categories
  • Camera with the task text on the video, saved on your device
What's included in Premium?
  • Unlimited tasks, with no daily cap

  • Future premium features at no extra cost
How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → KipFire → Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of your current paid period. It simply won't renew.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by KipFire. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you cancel without a refund, you keep Premium until your paid period ends. We don't give partial refunds for unused time.

When will I be charged?

Monthly plans renew every month and annual plans every year, on your subscription date. Cancel any time before then to stop future charges.

Account

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap Forgot Password, enter your email, and follow the reset link we send you.

Can I change my email address?

Not in the app yet. Email support@kipfire.com and we'll help.

Can I use KipFire on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with the same email, Google or Apple account. Premium follows your account.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Menu → Delete Account and confirm. Your personal data is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. This can't be undone.

Privacy

What data do you collect?

Only what the app needs: your email (if you sign in), a username if you set one, your language, how many tasks you've used, and your subscription status. We don't collect your videos, your precise location or your card number. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Where are my videos stored?

Only on your phone. KipFire doesn't upload or store your videos. They save to your photo gallery, and you control them.

Do you sell my data?

No. We don't sell, rent or share your personal information for marketing.

Using the app

How do I record a video?

Choose a category, get a task, tap the camera icon and record. The task text appears on your video and the video saves to your gallery.

Can I edit the text on my video?

You can move the task card around on the video. Changing the text itself isn't available yet.

Are tasks unique to me?

Cards are written for the category you pick. Some come from a shared pool, so someone else may get a similar one. You can always skip a card.

What if I don't like a task?

Tap Next for another one.

How do I change the app language?

Go to Menu → Language. Tasks are available in English and Spanish.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad. Android is coming soon.

Categories won't load or the app crashes. What do I do?

Check your internet connection, update to the latest version and restart the app. If it keeps happening, email support@kipfire.com with your device model, iOS version and what you were doing.

The camera doesn't work.

Go to Settings → KipFire and turn on Camera and Microphone, then restart the app.

How do I contact support?

Email support@kipfire.com. We aim to reply within 48 hours. Feature ideas are welcome too.

How it works

Four steps. No script, no editing, no planning.

  1. Open KipFirePick a category, or let For You mix formats for your niche.
  2. Get a random cardDon't like it? Tap Next until one clicks. Save the good ones for later.
  3. Film your reactionThe card text sits on your video, and you can move it. The video saves to your gallery.
  4. Post itShare from your camera roll to Reels, TikTok or YouTube Shorts.

Six kinds of cards

Each one is a different way into a video. Try them all and keep what feels like you.

Funny

A short funny line to act out or react to.

Challenges

A real attempt with an unsure ending. You decide whether to try it.

Questions

One question you answer on camera.

Would You Rather

Two choices people will argue about in your comments.

Hot Take

A short opinion someone will disagree with out loud.

Inner Me

An honest line about you, not a slogan.

Plus For You, which mixes all six formats around a niche you pick. Cards come in English and Spanish.

Questions people ask

How do I get content ideas every day?

Use a prompt instead of waiting for inspiration. KipFire gives you one short card to film: a question, a challenge, a hot take, a would-you-rather, or something funny. Tap for a new one whenever you need it, react on camera, and post.

I'm scared to film myself. What do I do?

Don't try to perform. Answer one question out loud, like you would to a friend. A card gives you the first line, so you only have to react. Most people find the second video much easier than the first.

Does KipFire make the video for me?

No. KipFire gives you the idea. You film it yourself, so the video is really you. The card text goes on your video and it saves to your phone.

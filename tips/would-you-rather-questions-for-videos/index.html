100 Would You Rather Questions for Videos

Would You Rather questions are one of the easiest video formats. They are short, everyone has an opinion, and viewers answer in the comments. Here are 100 questions you can film today. They are clean and friendly, so they work for any audience.

How to film a Would You Rather video

1. Answer fast. Show the question as text on screen and say your choice in the first two seconds. Then explain why in one or two sentences.

2. Ask the viewer. End with "What would you pick?" People love to disagree, and comments help the video reach more people.

3. Make it a series. Post one question a day, or answer five in one video. Ask a friend or your partner to answer too, and compare.

Questions people ask

What are good Would You Rather questions for TikTok?
The best ones are quick to read, easy to answer in one word, and a little hard to choose. For example: "Would you rather fly or teleport?" or "Would you rather have 1 million followers or 1,000 true fans?"

How do I film a Would You Rather video?
Put the question on screen as text, say your answer in the first seconds, explain why in one or two sentences, then ask viewers to pick in the comments.

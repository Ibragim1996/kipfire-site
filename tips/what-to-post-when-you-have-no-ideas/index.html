What to Post When You Have No Ideas: 5 Quick Fixes

You open your phone, you want to post, and your head is empty. This is normal. It is not a talent problem. "Come up with something" is just a hard job, because it is so open. These five fixes make the job smaller.

1. Answer a question instead of inventing a topic

"What should I post?" is too open. A question is easier. Pick one and answer it for 30 seconds:

  • What is a small thing that made your day better this week?
  • What is something you changed your mind about?
  • What is the worst advice you ever got?

Someone asks, you answer. No planning needed.

2. React to something

Reacting is easier than creating. Watch a short video, read a comment, or look at a headline. Then say what you think in one or two sentences. Your opinion and your face are enough to make a video.

3. Share one small lesson from today

You do not need to be an expert. What did you learn this week? How did you fix a small problem? "I tried this, here is what happened" is a complete video.

4. Use a Would You Rather or a light hot take

Two options make it easy to talk. "Would you rather have free coffee forever or free parking forever? Why?" Friendly hot takes about food, habits and daily life start comments, because everyone has an opinion.

5. Lower the bar: film 60 seconds, then decide

Press record, answer, and watch it back. You are allowed to delete it. This takes the pressure off, because filming and posting become two separate decisions.

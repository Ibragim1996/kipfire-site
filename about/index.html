About KipFire

KipFire gives you a short line to film. Pick a category, get a task, record it on your phone with the words on screen, and share it wherever you post.

What it is

KipFire is an iPhone and iPad app for people who want a concrete idea for their next video. It's built for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. It doesn't make content for you. It gives you the prompt, and you make something real with it.

Categories

  • Funny: a short funny line for a video.
  • Challenges: a real attempt with an unsure ending, written so you know what to try. You decide whether to do it.
  • Questions: one question you can answer on camera.
  • Would You Rather: two choices people can actually argue about.
  • Hot Take: a short opinion someone can disagree with out loud.
  • Inner Me: an honest line about you, not a slogan.
  • For You: all six formats, focused on a niche you pick.

How it works

  • Choose a category.
  • Get a line. The app writes one short task in English or Spanish.
  • Record. The camera puts that line on your video as a card you can move. The file stays on your phone.
  • Share. Post it from your camera roll wherever you already post.

Free and Premium

You can start for free. Guests get 5 tasks a day in Funny and Challenges. A free account gets 10 tasks a day and all six categories. Premium removes the daily limit. Plan details are in the FAQ.

Create responsibly

You're responsible for the content you create and post. KipFire provides ideas and tools, but we're not responsible for content that harms you or others, violates someone's rights, breaks platform rules or laws, or is offensive or misleading. Skip any card that doesn't feel right. Challenges are always your choice.

Your privacy

  • Your videos stay on your device. We don't upload or store them.
  • We collect only what the app needs to work.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the app menu.

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for details.

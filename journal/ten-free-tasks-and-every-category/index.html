A free account now includes 10 tasks a day and every category

A free KipFire account now covers more of the app.

What a free account includes

You get 10 tasks a day. Those 10 can come from any category: Funny, Challenges, Questions, Would You Rather, Hot Take, or Inner Me. It is one shared limit, not 10 from each category.

What guests get

Without an account, you get 5 tasks a day, and only Funny and Challenges. After the fifth task, the app offers a free account for the full 10 and the rest of the categories.

What Premium is for

Premium removes the daily limit. The categories themselves are open on a free account.

The allowance refreshes on a rolling window, not at midnight.

Want a new prompt every time you open your phone? KipFire gives you one card to film.
